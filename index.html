Veganuary in Greece · since 2024

Every January, you give hundreds of people their first vegan month. In Greek. For free.

In 2024, Vegan Life brought the international Veganuary challenge to Greece for the first time and built the only Greek-language version of it: 31 days of daily guidance, written for the Greek kitchen, the Greek supermarket and the Greek family table. In January 2026, its third year, 1,085 people signed up, 35 % more than the year before, and the campaign reached more than 2,000,000 people across Greece.

Nobody pays to take part. Someone pays to make it exist. In 2027, that someone can be you.

Here is what every participant receives, thanks to people like you:

  • 31 daily emails and 31 daily videos in Greek with Mediterranean recipes, local alternatives and everything needed for an easy transition
  • A free starter e-book written exclusively in Greek, with recipes and answers
  • A personal mentor and a support group for the whole month
  • Free cooking workshops and expert talks by chefs, doctors and nutritionists, with Greek Sign Language interpretation
  • Vegan options where people already eat and shop through 86 partner businesses, from nationwide pizza and coffee chains to supermarkets, and a Vegan Info Point with trained volunteers for any question

A few things you might wonder

Good questions.

Where exactly does my money go?

Into the free things: writing and sending the Greek Veganuary emails, updating the free e-book, training mentors and Info Point volunteers, preparing and delivering school and university workshops, producing educational videos and articles, and keeping entry to Vegan Life's events free. Your support also gives the team the ability to plan these months ahead instead of waiting for a grant.

Why monthly, and why now?

Because the next Veganuary is planned now, and school visits are booked months ahead. Steady monthly support is the only kind that lets anyone promise a school, a mentor or a participant that the help will be there. And the sooner the 35 are complete, the sooner 2027 can be planned with you in it.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Open your support management page on Stripe, sign in with the email you used, and choose cancel subscription. Your support ends at the end of the period you already paid for, with no further charge.

What if I can't afford a monthly donation right now?

That is often how the next participant, and the next supporter, arrives.

No. If you believe people deserve honest information and free help to make a kinder choice, you belong here. Many of the 35 are still on their own journey.
